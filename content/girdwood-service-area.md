## Overview

The Girdwood Valley Service Area (GVSA) is a special service area within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] that provides local services to [[girdwood]] residents. It is governed by the [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]].

## Funding

The GVSA is funded through a property tax mill levy assessed on properties within the service area boundary. This provides revenue for local services beyond what the Municipality provides to all areas.

## Services

The GVSA funds local services including parks and recreation, trail maintenance, fire and emergency services, contracted police services through the [[whittier-police-department]], and community facilities.

## Relationship to the Municipality

As a service area within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]], the GVSA operates under municipal authority. The [[anchorage-assembly]] has final approval over the GVSA budget and service area boundaries.
