## Overview

The Anchorage Assembly is the 12-member legislative body of the [[municipality-of-anchorage]], with 6 districts across Anchorage, each electing 2 members to staggered three-year terms ([Municipality of Anchorage — Assembly 101](https://www.muni.org/Departments/Assembly/Pages/Assembly-101.aspx)).

## Authority

The Assembly has authority over municipal ordinances and regulations, the annual budget, land use and zoning decisions, confirmation of mayoral appointments, and service area budgets including the [[girdwood-service-area]].
