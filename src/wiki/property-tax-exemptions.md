## Overview

Property tax is the primary funding source for most [[municipality-of-anchorage]] service areas, including Girdwood's [[girdwood-service-area]]. The Municipality offers several exemptions that reduce the assessed value subject to tax. Most must be applied for through the Property Appraisal Division by **March 15** of the applicable year ([source](../sources/anchorage-property-tax-exemptions.md)).

## Muni-wide Exemptions

The main exemptions available to Girdwood property owners ([source](../sources/anchorage-property-tax-exemptions.md)):

- **Senior citizen** — up to **$150,000** of assessed value exempted for owner-occupants aged 65+ (or 60+ surviving spouses of a qualified senior), on a primary residence.
- **Disabled veteran** — up to **$150,000** for veterans with a service-connected disability rated 50%+ (or their 60+ surviving spouse), on a primary residence.
- **Residential** — **40% of assessed value, up to $75,000**, on owner-occupied residential property.
- **Military service widow/widower** — up to **$150,000** for qualifying survivors; expires on remarriage.
- **Farm use land** — for land producing at least **$2,500** in agricultural products (Schedule F documentation).
- **Disaster** — for property at least 50% destroyed by fire; must apply **within 120 days**.
- **Nonprofit** — religious, charitable, cemetery, hospital, and educational properties; hospitals require annual certification.
- **Business personal property** — **$100,000** of assessed value, one per multi-location business.
- **Housing incentive** — for new construction of eight or more residential units; applied for before the Conditional Certificate of Occupancy.

The residential and senior/veteran exemptions stack where the owner qualifies, so an owner-occupied Girdwood home held by a senior can combine the residential and senior exemptions against its assessed value.

## Girdwood-specific Exemptions

There are none currently. I, Nick, might like to see some, such as even more aggressive residential exemptions,
so that girdwood residents pay a smaller fraction of the girdwood taxes, with the anchorage residents with [[dark-homes]] picking up the slack.

I'm not sure if it is possible/legal for [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]] to create these girdwood-specific exemptions.
If I had to guess, they might be able to propose it, but the [[anchorage-assembly]] would have to ultimately
approve it.
