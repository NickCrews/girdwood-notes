## Overview

The Girdwood Board of Supervisors (GBOS) is the local elected advisory board for [[girdwood]]. It serves as the primary voice of the community within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] government structure.

## Structure

GBOS consists of 5 members elected by Girdwood residents to staggered three-year terms ([Municipality of Anchorage — GBOS](https://www.muni.org/Departments/Assembly/Pages/GirdwoodBoardofSupervisors.aspx)).

## Role and authority

GBOS is an advisory body — it makes recommendations to the [[anchorage-assembly]] and the Mayor but does not have independent legislative or taxing authority. Its responsibilities include:

- Advising on land use and zoning decisions in Girdwood
- Managing the budget of the [[girdwood-service-area]]
- Providing community input on municipal decisions affecting Girdwood
- Overseeing local services funded through the GVSA
