# Property Tax Exemptions — Municipality of Anchorage

Most exemptions must be applied for by **March 15 of the applicable year**.
Property Appraisal Division, City Hall, 632 West 6th Avenue, Suite 300;
phone (907) 343-6770.

## Senior Citizen Exemption

- Exempts up to **$150,000 of assessed value**.
- Owner must be **65 or older** (on or before December 31 of the prior year), or
  a resident **60+** who is the widow/widower of a person who qualified as a senior.
- Must be owned and occupied as the **primary residence and permanent place of abode**.
- Deadline: **March 15**.

## Disabled Veteran Exemption

- Exempts up to **$150,000 of assessed value**.
- For veterans with a **service-connected disability rated 50% or more** (current
  VA letter, dated within one year, required), or their **60+** widow/widower.
- Must be the primary residence.
- Deadline: **March 15**.

## Residential Exemption

- Exempts **40% of assessed value, up to a $75,000 maximum**.
- For owner-occupied residential property.
- Deadline: **March 15**.

## Military Service Widow/Widower Exemption

- Exempts up to **$150,000 of assessed value** for the primary residence of
  qualifying survivors.
- Expires upon remarriage.
- Deadline: **March 15**.

## Farm Use Land Exemption

- Requires farming activity producing at least **$2,500 of agricultural products**,
  documented with a federal Schedule F.
- Deadline: **March 15**.

## Disaster Exemption

- For property at least **50% destroyed by fire** (or other disaster).
- Application required **within 120 days** of the event.

## Nonprofit / Community-Purpose Exemption

- For religious, charitable, cemetery, hospital, or educational properties.
- Hospital properties require **annual certification by March 15**.
- Initial deadline: **March 15**.

## Business Personal Property Exemption

- Exempts **$100,000 of assessed value**.
- Only one exemption per multi-location business.

## Housing Incentive (new construction)

- Requires a minimum of **eight residential units**.
- Application due **before the Conditional Certificate of Occupancy**.
