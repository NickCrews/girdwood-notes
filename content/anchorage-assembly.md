## Overview

The Anchorage Assembly is the legislative body of the [[municipality-of-anchorage]]. It consists of 11 members representing districts across the Municipality, serving three-year staggered terms.

## Role

The Assembly has authority over:

- Municipal ordinances and regulations
- The annual municipal budget
- Land use and zoning decisions
- Confirmation of mayoral appointments
- Service area budgets, including the [[girdwood-service-area]]

## Girdwood representation

Girdwood is part of an Assembly district that includes other communities south of Anchorage along Turnagain Arm. Because Girdwood is a small fraction of its district's population, the community's representation on the Assembly is limited.

This dynamic is a key reason the [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]] exists — to provide a local advisory voice on issues affecting [[girdwood]], even though final authority rests with the Assembly.

## Key decisions affecting Girdwood

The Assembly votes on matters critical to Girdwood, including:

- [[heritage-land-bank]] land disposals in the Girdwood Valley
- [[short-term-rentals]] regulations
- [[bed-tax]] rates and allocation
- Policing contracts (see [[whittier-police-department]])
