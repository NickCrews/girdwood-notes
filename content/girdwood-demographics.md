## Overview

[[Girdwood]] has a year-round population of approximately 1,800–2,300 people, according to U.S. Census estimates. The community's population swells significantly during ski season and summer tourism months due to seasonal workers.

## Population trends

Girdwood's population has remained relatively stable over recent decades, constrained by limited housing availability rather than lack of demand. The community is small relative to the broader [[municipality-of-anchorage]] (population ~290,000).

## Housing occupancy

A defining demographic feature of Girdwood is the high percentage of housing units that are not occupied as primary residences. Census data shows that over half of housing units in Girdwood are either vacant, used seasonally, or held as second homes. See [[dark-homes]] for detailed analysis.

This pattern distorts traditional demographic measures and makes Girdwood's "effective" community smaller than the housing stock would suggest.

## Workforce

The local workforce is heavily influenced by seasonal employment at [[alyeska-resort]] and other tourism-dependent businesses. See [[girdwood-economics-and-labor-market]] for details.

Many workers who serve Girdwood's economy commute from Anchorage or other communities because they cannot find affordable housing locally. See [[rental-market]] and [[homebuying-market]].
