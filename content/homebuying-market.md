## Overview

The homebuying market in [[girdwood]] is characterized by limited inventory and competition from second-home buyers and investors.

## Demand drivers

Multiple demand sources compete for Girdwood's limited housing stock:

- **Second-home buyers**: Purchasers who want a vacation property near [[alyeska-resort]]
- **Investors**: Buyers who plan to operate [[short-term-rentals]]
- **Year-round residents**: Workers and families who want to live in Girdwood full-time

Because second-home buyers and investors often have greater purchasing power than local workers, they can outbid residents in a tight market.

## Policy responses

- **[[Deed restrictions]]**: Requiring primary residency on new developments built on [[heritage-land-bank]] land
- **Workforce housing**: Proposed developments like Holtan Hills aim to create housing affordable to local workers
