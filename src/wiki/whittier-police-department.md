## Challenges

The arrangement presents logistical challenges. Whittier is accessible only through the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel, a single-lane tunnel that operates on a schedule, which can delay response times. Coverage levels and staffing have been ongoing concerns, and the community has debated whether alternative policing arrangements would better serve Girdwood.
