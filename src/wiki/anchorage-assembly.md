## Overview

The Anchorage Assembly is the 12-member legislative body of the [[municipality-of-anchorage]].
There are 6 districts across Anchorage, each with 2 members, each serving 3 year terms.
For more info see [the assembly page](https://www.muni.org/Departments/Assembly/Pages/Assembly-101.aspx).

## Authority

The Assembly has authority over municipal ordinances and regulations, the annual budget, land use and zoning decisions, confirmation of mayoral appointments, and service area budgets including the [[girdwood-service-area]].

## Girdwood representation

Girdwood is part of Assembly district 6, that includes South Anchorage down to Girdwood.

## Key decisions affecting Girdwood

The Assembly votes on matters critical to Girdwood, including [[heritage-land-bank]] land disposals, [[short-term-rentals]] regulations, [[bed-tax]] rates and allocation, and policing contracts (see [[whittier-police-department]]).
