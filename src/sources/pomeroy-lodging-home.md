Welcome to Pomeroy Lodging. We are fiercely invested.

At Pomeroy Lodging, we have invested in a diverse portfolio of proprietary brands to meet our guests’ specific needs.

Our investment goes beyond the walls of our hotels, and the land we build on. Through our skin-in-the-game philosophy, family-oriented business culture, and significant support of the communities in which we operate, we are fiercely invested in our people, our guests, our partners, and our communities.

### [Committed to the Community](http://pomeroylodging.com/about-us/community/)

Pomeroy is committed to investing into and being ambassadors of the communities we’re located in. We forge strategic partnerships and provide sponsorships to build strong, sustainable communities.

[More](http://pomeroylodging.com/about-us/community/) 

### [Committed to Growth](http://pomeroylodging.com/about-us/invest-in-pomeroy-lodging/)

In the last 10 years, Pomeroy Lodging has completed over $275M in new hotel development projects. As a vertically integrated developer, we leverage our vision and expertise throughout the construction process to ensure prosperities are up to the Pomeroy standard for our guests and team members.

[More](http://pomeroylodging.com/about-us/invest-in-pomeroy-lodging/)
