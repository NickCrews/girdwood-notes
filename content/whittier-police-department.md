## Overview

The Whittier Police Department (WPD) provides contracted police services to [[girdwood]] through an agreement with the [[municipality-of-anchorage]]. This arrangement has been in place since 2016.

## Background

Before 2016, Girdwood received police services from Alaska State Troopers. When the Troopers withdrew coverage due to state budget cuts, the community was left without dedicated law enforcement.

The [[municipality-of-anchorage]] subsequently contracted with the City of Whittier — a small city located about 25 miles from Girdwood through the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel — to provide police patrols and response in Girdwood.

## Funding

Policing services are funded through the [[girdwood-service-area]] budget, which is overseen by the [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]] and approved by the [[anchorage-assembly]].

## Challenges

The arrangement presents logistical challenges:

- Whittier is accessible only through a single-lane tunnel that operates on a schedule, potentially delaying response times
- Coverage levels and staffing have been ongoing concerns
- The community has debated whether alternative policing arrangements would better serve Girdwood
