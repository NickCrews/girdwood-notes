## Overview

[[Girdwood]] is a small community within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]]. The 2020 Census recorded a population of 2,361 for the Girdwood CDP ([Census 2020](https://data.census.gov/profile/Girdwood_CDP,_Alaska?g=160XX00US0227940)).

## Housing occupancy

A defining demographic feature of Girdwood is the high percentage of housing units that are not occupied as primary residences. See [[dark-homes]] for detailed analysis. This pattern makes Girdwood's "effective" resident community smaller than the housing stock would suggest.

## Workforce

The local workforce is heavily influenced by employment at [[alyeska-resort]] and other tourism-dependent businesses. See [[girdwood-economics-and-labor-market]].

Many workers who serve Girdwood's economy commute from Anchorage or other communities due to limited affordable housing locally. See [[long-term-rentals]] and [[homebuying-market]].
