## Overview

Girdwood is an unincorporated community within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]], located approximately 40 miles south of downtown Anchorage along the Seward Highway at the head of Turnagain Arm ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girdwood,_Anchorage,_Alaska)). It is the home of [[alyeska-resort]].

## History

Originally called "Glacier City," Girdwood was founded as a supply camp for placer gold miners. It was renamed for Colonel James Girdwood, who staked the first four gold claims along Crow Creek in 1896 ([Girdwood Area Plan](https://www.muni.org/Departments/OCPD/Planning/Documents/GAP2-LocationHistory.pdf)).

The 1964 Good Friday earthquake caused the original townsite to subside, and the town was relocated 2.5 miles up the valley to its current location ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girdwood,_Anchorage,_Alaska)).

Girdwood was incorporated as a city in 1961. In 1975, it was absorbed into the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] when the Greater Anchorage Area Borough unified with Anchorage and the borough's smaller cities.

## Governance

Girdwood is governed as part of the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] but has local representation through the [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]] (GBOS) and the [[girdwood-service-area]] (GVSA). The community is represented on the [[anchorage-assembly]] as part of a district that includes other areas south of Anchorage.

## Key issues

- **Housing**: A large share of homes are not occupied as primary residences. See [[dark-homes]], [[short-term-rentals]], [[rental-market]], [[homebuying-market]].
- **Economic dependence on tourism**: [[alyeska-resort]] and seasonal recreation drive the economy. See [[girdwood-economics-and-labor-market]].
- **Land use**: The [[heritage-land-bank]] holds significant acreage in the Girdwood Valley. The Holtan Hills development is a major pending project.
- **Policing**: Contracted to the [[whittier-police-department]] since 2016 after Alaska State Troopers withdrew.
