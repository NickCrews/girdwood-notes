## Personas and groups

Multiple groups are competing for buying a home in girdwood:

- **Homeowning residents**: Workers and families who already own a home in girdwood and are looking for something new.
- **Renting residents**: Younger and poorer folks who live here, but want a more stable foothold in the community.
- **Prospective residents**: people and families in anchorage and beyond who want to move to Girdwood full time.
- **Anchorage Second-home buyers**: Anchoragites who want a vacation property near [[alyeska-resort]]. Often come down on weekends, are more of a staple of the community.
- **Remote Second-home buyers**: other people who want a vacation property near [[alyeska-resort]]. Come to girdwood less often, less part of the community.
