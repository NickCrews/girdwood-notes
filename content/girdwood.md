## Overview

Girdwood is an unincorporated community within the [[municipality-of-anchorage]], located approximately 40 miles south of downtown Anchorage along the Seward Highway. It sits at the head of Turnagain Arm in the Chugach Mountains, within the northernmost extent of temperate rainforest on Earth, and serves as the closest access point to Chugach National Forest from Anchorage.

Girdwood is best known as the home of [[alyeska-resort]], Alaska's largest ski area. The community has a year-round population of roughly 1,800 people, but its population swells significantly with seasonal workers and tourists.

## History

Originally called "Glacier City," Girdwood was founded as a supply camp for placer gold miners along the creeks feeding Turnagain Arm. It was renamed for Colonel James Girdwood, a Belfast-born entrepreneur who staked the first four gold claims along Crow Creek in 1896.

The 1964 Good Friday earthquake caused the land under the original townsite to subside into Turnagain Arm. The town was relocated 2.5 miles up the valley to its current location.

Girdwood was incorporated as a city in 1961. In 1975, it was absorbed into the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] when the Greater Anchorage Area Borough unified with Anchorage and the borough's smaller cities.

## Governance

Girdwood is governed as part of the [[municipality-of-anchorage]] but has local representation through the [[girdwood-board-of-supervisors]] (GBOS) and the [[girdwood-service-area]] (GVSA). The community is represented on the [[anchorage-assembly]] as part of a district that includes other areas south of Anchorage.

## Key issues

- **Housing crisis**: Over half of homes are not occupied as primary residences. See [[dark-homes]], [[short-term-rentals]], [[rental-market]], [[homebuying-market]].
- **Economic dependence on tourism**: [[alyeska-resort]] and seasonal recreation drive the economy. See [[girdwood-economics-and-labor-market]].
- **Land use**: The [[heritage-land-bank]] holds approximately 5,000 acres in the Girdwood Valley. The Holtan Hills development is a major pending project.
- **Policing**: Contracted to the [[whittier-police-department]] since 2016 after Alaska State Troopers withdrew.
