## Overview

Alyeska Resort is Alaska's largest ski resort, located in [[girdwood]] at the base of Mount Alyeska in the Chugach Mountains. It is the primary economic engine of Girdwood and a major year-round tourist destination.

The resort is owned and operated by [[pomeroy]], a Canadian hospitality company that acquired it in 1980. The centerpiece is the Hotel Alyeska, a 304-room luxury hotel that opened in 1994.

## Skiing and recreation

Alyeska has 1,610 acres of skiable terrain with a vertical drop of 2,500 feet, serviced by 9 lifts including an aerial tram. The resort receives an average of 669 inches of snowfall per year.

In summer, the resort offers hiking, mountain biking, scenic tram rides, and other activities. The resort operates year-round and is a key employer in the valley.

## Economic role

Alyeska Resort is the largest employer in Girdwood and the primary driver of the local tourism economy. See [[girdwood-economics-and-labor-market]] for more detail.

The resort's seasonal employment patterns contribute significantly to Girdwood's housing challenges. Many seasonal workers struggle to find affordable housing, contributing to the [[rental-market]] crisis.

The resort also generates significant [[bed-tax]] revenue through its hotel operations.
